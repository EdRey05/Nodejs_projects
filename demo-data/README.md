# Fictional demonstration data

001/Synthetic_Portfolio.xlsx is the fuller Savings & Investing sample: 451 transactions across three generic accounts from January 2024 through October 25, 2026. All balances, prices, dates and activity are invented; real ticker symbols identify the asset categories only. No personal workbook rows were copied. portfolio-expected.json records the independently calculated expected totals.

Savings includes monthly deposits, four withdrawals and interest. Managed investing includes deposits and dividends without withdrawals. Self-directed investing includes deposits, purchases, sales, withdrawals, dividends, distributions, stock-lending income and cash interest. Its remaining holdings at cost are approximately 70% VTI, 10% AAPL, 10% MSFT and 10% O (REIT), excluding cash. The final balances are $25,399.89 savings, $32,802.48 managed and $65,905.67 self-directed ($11,305.66 cash plus $54,600.01 remaining average cost). Prices are fictional and the sample does not model live market value or tax accounting.

Use a copy of the sample when experimenting. Manage & Import → Open workbook links its actual Windows path: edits save to that same file and it reopens next launch. Export downloads a separate copy; Import a temporary copy retains the old upload/export workflow. The four sheets and row-one headers are the app's import schema; the legacy bank field contains generic Savings/Investing labels rather than institution names.

The older 001 workbooks below are internal regression fixtures, not packaged examples. 001/Synthetic_FinancialData.xlsx is the original input: three accounts, 30 transactions, savings balance 4290. All banks, brokers and assets are fictional. expected.json describes that input. 001/FinancialData.xlsx is the actual app export after adding one 100 contribution: 31 transactions, savings balance 4390. Import/export round-trip was checked through the real packaged UI.

002/Notebook.md is the original fictional note. 002/Notebook-edited.md is the actual saved/reopened editor output. Nothing here comes from a company, patient or personal account. Use copies when experimenting; the editor saves directly to the selected file.

Portable folders put supplied samples under examples/. Savings & Investing ships only Synthetic_Portfolio.xlsx; its expected-results JSON and legacy regression workbooks remain in the repository for tests. Repository links use demo-data/. Runtime and recording evidence is retained in Desktop/Nodejs_standarization and media receipts in Images_GIFs_Videos/recordings.json. No 003 demonstration data is published.
